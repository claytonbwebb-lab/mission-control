About Us

Quality & Craftsmanship You Can Trust

Restore My Door was established in 2014 with a passion for transforming spaces without the hefty price tag of full replacements. Based in Manchester, we serve customers across the North West, bringing our expertise to homes and businesses alike.

We pride ourselves on our attention to detail and offer a comprehensive service from our in-house workshop, equipped with state-of-the-art spray booths for a perfect finish every time. Our dedicated team of professionals are committed to delivering outstanding results and exceptional customer service.

Whether it's breathing new life into old kitchen cabinets with a fresh vinyl wrap, modernising UPVC windows with durable spray paint, or designing and fitting bespoke new doors, Restore My Door is your trusted partner for premium renovations. We believe in quality that lasts and a service that exceeds expectations.

Our experienced team, founded by industry veterans, combines decades of knowledge in carpentry, design, and finishing techniques to ensure every project meets our rigorous standards.
